A LITTLE FRIENDLY RIVALRY
THE FIELD GUIDE

A familiar game.
A friendly rival.

  1. Invite your people. Share your six-character room code or invite link. Rooms hold 2–8 players. Everyone taps Ready, then the host starts the shared three-second countdown.
  2. Choose your field. Easy: 9 rows × 9 columns, 10 mines. Medium: 16 × 16, 40 mines. Hard: 30 rows × 16 columns, 99 mines. The host can change the choice in the lobby; everyone must ready up again.
  3. Same field. Separate boards. All tiles start hidden, and mines are randomly scattered across the entire board. Everyone gets the same layout. Your first click can hit a mine. Numbers count neighboring mines, including diagonals.
  4. Mark what you suspect. Switch to Flag mode, then tap tiles to mark or unmark them. On desktop, right-click or press F on a focused tile.
  5. Clear it to win. The first player to reveal all safe tiles ends the race immediately. Flags don’t add points.
  6. A mine freezes your score. Remaining players keep racing. The last survivor wins once they pass every frozen score. If everyone hits a mine, the highest safe-tile count wins; tied top scores draw. A player who concedes cannot win.
  7. Big board, small screen. Use + and − to resize tiles or Fit to see the full width. Drag inside the board to scroll. Your browser’s pinch zoom still works.

Tap a revealed number to open its remaining neighbors when it has the right number of adjacent flags. Wrong flags can hit a mine. Random boards may require a guess.
