THE FIELD GUIDE

A familiar game.
A friendly rival.

  1. Invite your people. Share your six-character room code or invite link. Rooms hold 2–12 players. Everyone taps Ready, then the host starts the shared three-second countdown.
  2. Choose your field. Easy: 9 × 9, 10 mines. Medium: 16 × 16, 40 mines. Hard: 30 rows × 16 columns, 99 mines. In Custom, the host chooses 1–50 rows, 1–50 columns, and 0 mines up to the total tile count. Changing the board in the lobby resets everyone’s Ready.
  3. The Daily field. A solo Medium board shared by everyone. It resets at 12:00 AM fixed EST (UTC−5), year-round. Finish or end an attempt to copy results. Retries are counted in this browser and included in the message, with a little attitude.
  4. Same field. Separate boards. All tiles start hidden, and mines are randomly scattered across the entire board. Everyone gets the same layout. Your first click can hit a mine. Numbers count neighboring mines, including diagonals.
  5. Tap or hold. A short tap reveals a tile. Hold a covered tile still for about half a second to flag or unflag it. Dragging scrolls the board and cancels the hold. On desktop, right-click or press F on a focused tile.
  6. Clear it to win. The first player to reveal all safe tiles ends the race immediately. Flags don’t add points.
  7. A mine freezes your score. Remaining players keep racing. The last survivor wins once they pass every frozen score. If everyone hits a mine, the highest safe-tile count wins; tied top scores draw. A player who concedes cannot win.
  8. Big board, small screen. Use + and − to resize tiles or Fit to see the full width. Drag inside the board to scroll. Your browser’s pinch zoom still works.

Tap a revealed number to open its remaining neighbors when it has the right number of adjacent flags. Wrong flags can hit a mine. Random boards may require a guess.
