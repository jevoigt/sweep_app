A LITTLE FRIENDLY RIVALRY
THE FIELD GUIDE

A familiar game.
A friendly rival.

  1. Invite your person. Share your six-character room code or invite link. Both players tap Ready for the same three-second countdown.
  2. Same field. Separate boards. Your center opening is safe and identical. Tap a covered tile to reveal it. Numbers count neighboring mines, including diagonals.
  3. Mark what you suspect. Switch to Flag mode, then tap tiles to mark or unmark them. On desktop, right-click or press F on a focused tile.
  4. Clear it to win. The first player to reveal all safe tiles ends the race immediately. Flags don’t add points.
  5. A mine freezes your score. Your opponent keeps playing until they beat your safe-tile count or hit a mine. If they’re already ahead, they win immediately. If both hit mines, most safe tiles wins; equal scores draw.

Tap a revealed number to open its remaining neighbors when it has the right number of adjacent flags. Wrong flags can hit a mine. Random boards may require a guess.
